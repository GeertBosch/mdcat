Mermaid graphs that need several tracks
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer gives each bus of edges between two
ranks a track of its own, so edges that interleave no longer need to share one.
Where one edge must cross another, the horizontal stroke breaks to make a
bridge, so a crossing never looks like a join. Checked by mermaid-kitty.sh at
width 80, with --img kitty and pinned cell sizes. The .err golden names the
diagrams that are declined, and so are shown as source.

A dot always marks a split or a merge. A bridge has no dot.
