Mermaid graphs with branches and merges
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer draws any acyclic graph of boxes
joined by --> links (mermaid-cycles.md covers the graphs with cycles). The
graphs here are trees and merges that need no more than one track between two
ranks; mermaid-routing.md covers the ones that need more. Checked by
mermaid-kitty.sh at widths 80 and 44, with --img kitty and pinned cell sizes.
The .err goldens name the diagrams that are declined, for now, and so are shown
as source.

Where several edges leave one box they share its port and split off a common
bus; where several arrive they merge into one arrowhead. A dot marks each split
or merge, and a bend is rounded.
