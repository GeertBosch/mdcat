Mermaid node shapes
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer draws each node's outline as part of
the diagram's image, and its label as ordinary text over it. Besides the box,
[text], it draws the rounded box (text), the stadium ([text]), the circle
((text)), the double circle (((text))), the subroutine [[text]], the cylinder
[(text)], the rhombus {text} and the hexagon {{text}}, and the slanted shapes:
the parallelograms [/text/] and [\text\], the trapezoids [/text\] and [\text/],
and the flag >text]. The same shapes can be named with A@{ shape: name, label:
"text" }. The curves and slants are drawn exactly, so an arrowhead touches them
exactly where an edge meets them, even off the middle of a side. Each shape
takes the room its outline needs to clear the label: the stadium, subroutine and
hexagon are 2 columns wider than a box, the cylinder is a row taller (its label
sits under the lid) and at least as wide as an 8-column label, so that its lid
looks equally deep whatever the label, the rhombus has the label's rows

  ●   4 rows and about twice its width, a circle is widened until the label's
      box fits inside, a parallelogram or trapezoid is 2 columns wider than a
      box with its sides slanted by a column, and the flag is a column wider,
      with a notch a column deep in its left side. Checked by mermaid-kitty.sh
      at width 80, with --img kitty and pinned cell sizes.

Every shape, top down
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: seven shapes in a column, joined by arrows that end exactly on each
outline: a box, a rounded box with corners of half a row, a stadium with
semicircular ends, a circle (really an ellipse), a double circle with two
outlines a few pixels apart, a subroutine with a vertical line half a cell
inside each side, and a cylinder with a lid. The label of each is centred; the
cylinder's is below its lid.

rounded, stadium, circle, dbl-circ, fr-rect, cyl, diam, hex, lean-r, lean-l,
trap-b, trap-t and odd, and the aliases that Mermaid gives them (event, pill,
db, decision, prepare, in-out, out-in, priority, manual). A node with no label
is labelled with its id, and a label may be quoted, hold syntax characters and
be a Markdown string. Other keys are ignored.
