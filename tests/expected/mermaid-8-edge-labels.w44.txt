Mermaid edge labels
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer
writes an edge's label as ordinary text on
the edge, which stops a little short of it
on either side. The label is a node of its
own in the layout, halfway along the edge,
so it gets room like any other box. Every
rank is twice as far apart in a diagram with
labels, so a label always has a rank to sit
in. A label doesn't stop its edge from
sharing a port: edges with different labels,
or none, still split from one box or merge
into one on a single bus. Checked by
mermaid-kitty.sh at widths 80 and 44, with
--img kitty and pinned cell sizes. The .err
golden names the diagrams that are declined,
and so are shown as source. A diagram too
wide for width 44 is drawn wider than it,
after fitting (see mermaid-12-fit.md) has
done what it can.

Every syntax (top down)
────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: each pair is joined as its label
says, and the label sits on the edge midway
between the two boxes. -- text --> and
-->|text| draw the same arrow, and --- and
-- text --- the same open link. The dotted
link is dashed, the thick one is thick with
a larger arrowhead, and --o and --x end in a
circle and an X. A space between --> and |
makes no difference.

Brackets and pipes in link text (top down)
────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: text in the middle of a link is
everything up to the link's own end, so
parentheses, brackets, braces and a pipe are
plain text there (foo(), [5], {n}, a|b), and
the label is drawn on the edge as written.
(After --> or ---, a |text| label may not
hold them: Mermaid rejects that, and see
Declined.)

Declined
────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: shown as source, as the .err
golden says, in this order. A label with a
single word over 24 columns is too wide, as
that cannot be wrapped; a label with HTML
other than a line break, or an invisible
link with a label, is not drawn. A |text|
label with parentheses or brackets is a
parse error in Mermaid, and so is --x-->,
where --x is a link end. Text that touches
the end of its link (-- foo--> ) is read by
Mermaid as fo with the o taken as a marker,
so it is declined rather than drawn as foo.
(Quoted and Markdown labels are drawn:
mermaid-9-label-text.md.)

  graph TD
      a[A] -->|internationalization_and_localization_of_labels| b[B]

  graph TD
      a[A] -->|<b>bold</b>| b[B]

  graph TD
      a[A] ~~~|hidden| b[B]

  graph TD
      a[A] -->|foo()| b[B]

  graph TD
      a[A] -->|a[b]| b[B]

  graph TD
      a[A] --x--> b[B]

  graph TD
      a[A] -- foo--> b[B]
