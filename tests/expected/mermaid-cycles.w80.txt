Mermaid cycles
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer draws a graph with cycles. Like
Dagre, it finds the edges that close a cycle with a depth-first search, in order
of first appearance, and reverses them for the layout, so that the graph is laid
out in ranks. Each of those back edges is then drawn from its real source to its
real target, against the flow: it leaves the top of its source (top down), runs
up, and arrives, with its arrowhead, at the bottom of its target. It has ports
of its own, one cell off the middle of the side, so it never shares strokes with
the edges that run the other way. Checked by mermaid-kitty.sh at width 80, with
--img kitty and pinned cell sizes. The .err golden names the diagrams that are
declined, and so are shown as source.
