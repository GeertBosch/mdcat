Mermaid node shapes
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer draws each node's outline as part of
the diagram's image, and its label as ordinary text over it. Besides the box,
[text], it draws the rounded box (text), the stadium ([text]), the circle
((text)), the double circle (((text))), the subroutine [[text]], the cylinder
[(text)], the rhombus {text} and the hexagon {{text}}. The curves and slants are
drawn exactly, so an arrowhead touches them exactly where an edge meets them,
even off the middle of a side. Each shape takes the room its outline needs to
clear the label: the stadium, subroutine and hexagon are 2 columns wider than a
box, the cylinder is a row taller (its label sits under the lid) and at least as
wide as an 8-column label, so that its lid looks equally deep whatever the
label, the rhombus has the label's rows

  ●   4 rows and about twice its width, and a circle is widened until the
      label's box fits inside. Checked by mermaid-kitty.sh at width 80, with
      --img kitty and pinned cell sizes.

Every shape, top down
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: seven shapes in a column, joined by arrows that end exactly on each
outline: a box, a rounded box with corners of half a row, a stadium with
semicircular ends, a circle (really an ellipse), a double circle with two
outlines a few pixels apart, a subroutine with a vertical line half a cell
inside each side, and a cylinder with a lid. The label of each is centred; the
cylinder's is below its lid.
