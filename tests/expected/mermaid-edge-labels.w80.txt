Mermaid edge labels
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer writes an edge's label as ordinary
text on the edge, which stops a little short of it on either side. The label is
a node of its own in the layout, halfway along the edge, so it gets room like
any other box. Every rank is twice as far apart in a diagram with labels, so a
label always has a rank to sit in. A labelled edge has its own port: it never
shares a bus. Checked by mermaid-kitty.sh at widths 80 and 44, with --img kitty
and pinned cell sizes. The .err golden names the diagrams that are declined, and
so are shown as source: at width 44, those too wide for it.

Every syntax (top down)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: each pair is joined as its label says, and the label sits on the edge
midway between the two boxes. -- text --> and -->|text| draw the same arrow, and
--- and -- text --- the same open link. The dotted link is dashed, the thick one
is thick with a larger arrowhead, and --o and --x end in a circle and an X. A
space between --> and | makes no difference.
