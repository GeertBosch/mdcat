Mermaid flowcharts drawn by mdcat
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a terminal with the Kitty
graphics protocol, a
```mermaid  block holding a
flowchart of boxes is drawn by
mdcat itself: the boxes and
arrows are one transparent
image, and the labels are
ordinary text in the cells
over it. Checked by
mermaid-kitty.sh, which
renders it with --img kitty
and pinned cell sizes. Other
terminals, and every diagram
outside this subset, behave as
before. mermaid-dag.md covers
the branching shapes in depth.

Left to right
──────────────────────────────

Expected: three boxes in a row
joined by arrows. Every
arrowhead touches the next
box's outline, and each label
is centred in its box.

  graph LR
      parse[Parse] --> render[Render]
      render --> output[Output]
