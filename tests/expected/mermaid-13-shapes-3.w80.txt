Mermaid node shapes III: divided, lined process, lined cylinder
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer draws three more shapes, named with
A@{ shape: name, label: "text" }, each a familiar shape with one extra stroke
inside its outline, as mmdc draws them:

  ●   the divided process div-rect (aliases div-proc, divided-process,
      divided-rectangle): a box with a horizontal line across it, a row below
      its top edge, so the box is a row taller and its label sits under the
      line;
  ●   the lined process lin-rect (aliases lin-proc, lined-process,
      lined-rectangle, shaded-process): a box with one vertical line half a cell
      inside its left side, 2 columns wider than a box, the label centred;
  ●   the lined cylinder lin-cyl (aliases disk, lined-cylinder): a cylinder with
      a second front arc a row below the lid's, so it is two rows taller than a
      box.

The extra line is part of the shape's interior: edges still meet the outer
outline exactly (an arrowhead touches it, as with a box, a subroutine or a
cylinder), a node's stroke colour colours the extra line too, and its fill fills
the whole outline. Checked by mermaid-kitty.sh at width 80, with --img kitty and
pinned cell sizes.

Every new shape, top down
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: a box, then the three shapes in a column, joined by arrows that end
exactly on each outline. The divided process has a line across it one row under
its top edge, with its label below the line. The lined process has a vertical
line just inside its left side, and its label is centred. The lined cylinder has
its lid and, a row under it, a second arc of the same curve; its label is below
both.
