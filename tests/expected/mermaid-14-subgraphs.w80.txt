subgraph ... end is read into the model (Graph::clusters, Node::cluster) and
drawn, in every direction but only one level deep
(docs/plan-mermaid-subgraphs.md, Steps 5-8): the box is one outline under the
nodes and edges, around the nodes named inside it, with the subgraph's title, if
it has one, in bold under the top border. The cases whose heading starts with
Drawn are drawn; the title says the direction where it is not top-down. The rest
are declined: a nested subgraph is subgraph layout for now, and the other cases
are declined for the reason named in their heading or, for the membership cases,
because of what they hold. MDCAT_MERMAID_DEBUG=1 prints each reason on stderr,
and mermaid-kitty.sh compares it with expected/mermaid-14-subgraphs.w80.err.
Which node belongs to which subgraph is checked outside the repository, with a
scratch parser.

A blank title (subgraph s[" "]) draws no title, so the first cases use it.
