subgraph ... end is read into the model (Graph::clusters, Node::cluster) and, so
far, drawn only top-down, one level deep and without a title
(docs/plan-mermaid-subgraphs.md, Steps 5-8): the box is one outline under the
nodes and edges, around the nodes named inside it. The cases whose heading
starts with Drawn are drawn. The rest are declined: a title, a nested subgraph
or a direction other than TD is subgraph layout for now, and the other cases are
declined by the parser for the reason named in their heading.
MDCAT_MERMAID_DEBUG=1 prints each reason on stderr, and mermaid-kitty.sh
compares it with expected/mermaid-14-subgraphs.w80.err. Which node belongs to
which subgraph is checked outside the repository, with a scratch parser.

A blank title (subgraph s[" "]) draws no title, so these cases use it.

MongoDB: admission control
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Mermaid source is the MongoDB diagram as written; its subgraphs have no
titles of their own, so the id is the title.

  flowchart TB
  A(Stable Probe) --> |at minimum and tickets not exhausted|A

  A --> |"(above minimum and tickets not exhausted) or at maximum"|C(Probe Down)
  subgraph decrease
  C --> |throughput increased|F{{Decrease stable concurrency}}
  C --> |throughput did not increase|G{{Go back to stable concurrency}}
  end
  F --> H
  G --> H

  A --> |below maximum and tickets exhausted| B(Probe Up)
  subgraph increase
  B --> |throughput increased|D{{Increase stable concurrency}}
  B --> |throughput did not increase|E{{Go back to stable concurrency}}
  end
  D --> H(Stable Probe)
  E --> H
