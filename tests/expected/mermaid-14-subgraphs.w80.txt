Mermaid subgraphs
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

subgraph ... end is read into the model (Graph::clusters, Node::cluster) and
drawn, in every direction, nested up to three levels deep
(docs/plan-mermaid-subgraphs.md, Steps 5-8): the box is one outline under the
nodes and edges, around the nodes named inside it and the boxes of the subgraphs
inside it, with the subgraph's title, if it has one, in bold under the top
border. The cases whose heading starts with Drawn are drawn; the title says the
direction where it is not top-down, and nested where a box holds another. The
rest are declined: nesting deeper than three levels is subgraph layout: depth,
and the other cases are declined for the reason named in their heading or, for
the membership cases, because of what they hold. MDCAT_MERMAID_DEBUG=1 prints
each reason on stderr, and mermaid-kitty.sh compares it with
expected/mermaid-14-subgraphs.w80.err. Which node belongs to which subgraph is
checked outside the repository, with a scratch parser.

A blank title (subgraph s[" "]) draws no title, so the first cases use it.
