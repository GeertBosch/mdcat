Mermaid link types and lengths
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

On a Kitty terminal, the built-in renderer draws every Mermaid link style:
arrows and open links, dotted and thick ones, invisible ones that only place
boxes, circle and cross ends, and markers at both ends. Checked by
mermaid-kitty.sh at width 80, with --img kitty and pinned cell sizes; the
quadrant preview is too coarse to show dashes, so mermaid-kitty.sh also reads a
dotted link's pixels.

Every link style (top down)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: each pair is joined as its label says. --> has a filled arrowhead and
--- has none; -.-> and -.- are dashed, the first with an arrowhead; ==> and ===
are thick, the first with a larger arrowhead; --o ends in a filled circle and
--x in an X. Nothing is drawn for ~~~, but its two boxes still sit one rank
apart.

thick forms.

Lengths
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Expected: a link spans one rank more for each extra character. --> spans one
rank, ---> and -..-> and ===> two, and ----> three; the open link ---- spans
two. Each long link keeps its style, dashes or thickness, along the whole way.
